statistics :-- the process of getting the data,organizing the data, interpreting data and projecting the data 

types statistics :
======================================
descriptive   :-- will only work with previous data i.e. will 	used to analyze the past data
		>>>we cannot predict new values 

measure of central tendency  :-- will convert the multiple data point into single data point
		>>>mean,median,mode 
measure of dispersion   :-- will used to spread the each and every point i.e. the points how much 			deviate from its original location
		>>>range,mean deviation,varience,standard deviation,iqr,covarience,skewness


inferential   :-- will used to predict new outcome 

testing        :-- will used to identify weather outcome is correct or not 
		>>>z-test,t-test,chi-Square test,ANOVA
correlation     :-- will check how strong connected the two variable
