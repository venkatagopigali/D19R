		>>>pie,histogram

		>>>line,bar,scatter 
multi variant  :-- will pass more then one continuous data and more then one categorical data
		>>>heat,tree,scatter, etc----

continuous data :-- the data in between the two range of values 
categorical data :-- will form data based on condition or the repeated data
